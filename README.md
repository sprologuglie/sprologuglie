# Hi, I'm Guglielmo 👋

I'm a philosophy of science researcher based in Turin, Italy.
My work sits at the intersection of philosophy and empirical research —
I use bibliometric and statistical methods to study how the philosophy
of science community produces and organizes knowledge.

## 🔬 Research interests

- Philosophy of science and scientometrics
- Epistemic networks and collective knowledge dynamics
- Diversity in academic research and its effects on epistemic outcomes

## 🛠 Tools & technologies

- **Python** — data analysis, statistical modeling, agent-based modeling
- **R** — regression models, bibliometrics
- **Jupyter Notebooks** — reproducible and readable research workflows

## 📂 Featured projects

- **[Interactive-Epistemic-ABM-Zollman-Using-Mesa](https://github.com/sprologuglie/Interactive-Epistemic-ABM-Zollman-Using-Mesa)**
  Replication and extension of classic epistemic network models
  (Zollman 2010, Rosenstock et al. 2017, Frey and Šešelja 2020) with an interactive dashboard.

- **[Air-Quality-and-EU-2030-OpenAQ](https://github.com/sprologuglie/Air-Quality-and-EU-2030-OpenAQ)**
  A reproducible data pipeline analyzing EU city-level air quality
  against the 2030 targets, using OpenAQ data.
